Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026.

This policy covers Bench: the website at bench.porous.systems and the Bench app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Bench collects as little as it can. There are no accounts, no advertising, no tracking across apps or sites, and no cookies. Below is everything that leaves your device, why, who handles it, and what you can ask of us.

Who is responsible

Vitgranen AB, Sweden, makes Bench and is the controller of the personal data described here. Write to bench@porous.systems about anything in this policy.

The apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac

  • The apps collect no data. There are no analytics, accounts or tracking. Your desk, each app's stored values and your settings stay on your device until you delete the app.
  • The relay. Only when you press Connect does a device join the Resident relay, under a random ID (sim-…). The apps pushed to that device and the events it sends then pass between the app and the relay, which is run by Inanimate (resident.inanimate.tech), or by whoever runs the relay you set in Settings. We don't receive that traffic. Inanimate's handling of it is theirs: see inanimate.tech.

As any web host does, Vercel processes the requests your browser makes (including your IP address) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Who handles data for us

  • Vercel hosts the site and counts page views (Vercel Web Analytics).
  • Upstash, through Vercel, stores short share links and the hour-long rate limit.

They act on our instructions. Both are US companies, so data may be processed outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under the safeguards they provide, such as the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses. Inanimate, which runs the Resident relay, handles relay traffic as its own service, not on our behalf. We don't sell data or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to personal data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to limit or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Since Bench has no accounts, we usually can't tell whose data is whose: to delete a short link, send us the link. Write to bench@porous.systems. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the authority where you live.

Children

Bench isn't directed at children and collects nothing that identifies anyone, of any age.

Changes

If what Bench does with data changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. Bench is open source, so every change is in its history: github.com/sjunnesson/porous-bench.
