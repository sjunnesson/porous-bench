porous.systems Bench

Privacy

Bench is the website at bench.porous.systems and the Bench app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Vitgranen AB. Updated 15 October 2026.

Bench collects as little as it can: no accounts, no advertising, no tracking, and no cookies. What follows is everything that leaves your device, and why.

The website

  • Bench runs in your browser. Your bench, its apps and your own code stay in the browser's storage on your device.
  • Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and records nothing about your apps or your bench. Vercel hosts the site and, as any host does, handles the requests your browser makes.
  • Share links. A long link carries your setup in the link itself; nothing is stored. A short link (bench.porous.systems/s/…) stores the setup you share, which can include your app's code, so that anyone with the link can open it. It's kept with no end date. To limit how many links one address makes (30 an hour), your IP address is held for up to an hour; it isn't kept with the setup.
  • Mirroring. When you mirror an app to a device, the app and the state of your bench's controls go to that device through the Resident relay, which Inanimate runs (resident.inanimate.tech).

The apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac

  • The apps collect no data: no analytics, no accounts, no tracking. Your desk, each app's stored values and your settings stay on the device.
  • The relay. Only when you press Connect does a device join the Resident relay, under a random ID (sim-…). The apps pushed to it and the events it sends then go between the app and the relay: Inanimate's, unless you set a relay of your own in Settings. Bench's maker doesn't receive that traffic.
